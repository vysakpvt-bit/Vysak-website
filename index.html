Who We Are

About Vysak

Vysak is an event management company based in Warangal, Telangana, built on one belief — a great event should feel effortless for the people it's thrown for.

We started with a small team and a large idea: that world-class event production shouldn't be limited to the big cities. Today we plan and execute weddings, corporate events, concerts, college fests, exhibitions and private celebrations across India — from intimate gatherings of fifty to full-scale productions for thousands.

What makes us different is how we work. One team, one point of contact, one plan — from the first call to the final clean-up. Venue, decor, sound, light, catering, hospitality and on-ground coordination all sit under a single roof, so you get to be a guest at your own event.

Every event we take on gets the same promise: a crew that shows up early, a run-sheet that's been checked twice, and a team that doesn't leave until everything is exactly right.
